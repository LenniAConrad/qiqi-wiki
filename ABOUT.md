# About the Voxelgame wiki

Use [Player guides](guides.html) for getting started, controls, survival and
crafting. The [feature ledger](features.html) records source evidence and guide
coverage. [Unwritten guides](guides.html#coming) are listed separately.

The biome, animal and island galleries retain their own dated
snapshots. Photographs come from the original game's renderer. Animal portraits
use production models; island panoramas are labelled offline terrain renders.
Coordinates and measurements describe the recorded builds.

Website code, original text, derived data and original images are distributed
under [GPL-3.0-only](LICENSE). [Provenance and licence notices](NOTICE.md)
describe the assets and their sources. No proprietary game artwork or
third-party fonts are included.

The website uses static files and relative links and can be opened offline.
The game runtime, saves, internal instructions, source patches and raw build
logs remain outside the published site.
