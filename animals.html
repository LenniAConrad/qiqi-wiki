01

The actual game models

These studio previews use Voxelgame’s production pose meshes and exported materials. Neutral lighting makes silhouettes and markings easier to see. They are static resting and moving poses rendered in Blender; they are not recordings of live animal behavior.

02

Where to look

Habitat lists come from the game’s ambient wildlife rules. Water depth, terrain and local population also affect spawning, so a listed biome is a place to look rather than a guaranteed sighting. Follow a habitat link for its landscape, world seed and map coordinates.

03

Small creatures, close up

Each animal is framed to fill its portrait. The dimensions in its details describe the resting model, including horns, tails and wings. Picture sizes do not compare the animals’ actual sizes.
