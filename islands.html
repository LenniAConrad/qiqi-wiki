01

Six families, many coastlines

Rounded, Long, Crescent, Twin-lobed, Rugged and Atoll describe island shapes. Each family varies from island to island, and an island can contain several biomes. These examples are a starting point for exploring the variety.

02

From map to horizon

Top-down maps and 4K panoramas cover the whole island. Panoramas render sampled game terrain offline at true vertical scale, beyond the in-game horizon; trees, caves and small structures are omitted. Temperature, moisture, elevation and ruggedness tabs show actual generator values. Their legends explain the units; temperature is not degrees Celsius.

03

Find the same place

Use the listed seed and generation version to reproduce an island. Coordinates mark its centre, which can lie in water. Planned long and short axes describe the island's scale; sampled dry land measures its area. Older saved worlds retain their original generation.
